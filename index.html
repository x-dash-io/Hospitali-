Afya Ventures — From Campus to Communities

The waiting room does the history-taking now.

Hospitali+ is a facility-based digital pre-consultation system. While a patient waits to be seen, they describe what's wrong in their own words — Kiswahili, icons, plain language. The clinician walks in to a structured history, not a blank page.

Why it holds up in a real OPD

Built for the gap between patient and clinician

Speaks the patient's language

Simple Kiswahili, icons, and body diagrams. No assumption of medical vocabulary, health literacy, or digital fluency.

Speaks the clinician's language

"Pain in the upper middle of the stomach" becomes Epigastric pain, 3 days, burning, 6/10 — ready for the notes.

Never diagnoses

Hospitali+ structures information and flags urgency. The clinical decision always stays with the clinician.

What changes

Less repetition, more consultation

Patient

Shorter effective wait, and a clinician who already understands why they came.

Clinician

Less time spent extracting a narrative, more time spent examining and deciding.

Facility

Better patient flow and documentation, without adding staff.
